MMRIA is the token of Memeria, a free-to-play browser RPG. This page says what MMRIA is, what it is not, and where every address lives, so you can check it yourself instead of trusting us.

Short version. You can play the whole game without MMRIA. The token never makes you stronger than someone who earns their gear by playing. There is no team allocation, no presale and no promise of price.

What MMRIA is for

Coins, earned by playing, buy everyday gear. MMRIA is for access and extras. This is the plan, and none of it is live yet:

  • Cosmetics: skins, auras, mascot palettes.
  • Recipes and materials that save time, not that add power.
  • Entry to weekly tournaments and events.

The rule: power is earned in the game. Anything that touches power stays reachable with coins and effort. The special weapons currently shown as "Soon" will only go on sale once this rule is settled.

Where the money goes

  • Creator fees from pump.fun go to the creator wallet listed above.
  • They pay for servers, the domain and development of the game.
  • MMRIA paid inside the game goes to the weekly ranking prize pool or is burned. It is not kept by us.

Each time the treasury moves funds, the transaction is public. We will summarise it here.

How to verify

  • Look up the mint on RugCheck and on Solscan.
  • Confirm that mint and freeze authority are revoked.
  • Check the top holders. The creator wallet should be small.
  • Compare every address with this page.

A brand-new token will always show a few "new / low liquidity" warnings. That is normal and goes away as it grows.

What we will not do

  • Promise price, returns or "the next 100x".
  • Ask for your seed phrase. Ever.
  • Mint more supply, or add a hidden tax or freeze.
